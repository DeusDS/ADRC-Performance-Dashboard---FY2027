Capacity gap estimate

Staffing Needed to Clear the Callback Backlog

Illustrative estimate. Adjust the assumptions; defaults come from the selected period.

Additional FTE = backlog ÷ (calls one FTE handles per day × working days to clear). This sizes temporary capacity for the existing backlog only; it does not model new demand, absences or skill mix, and is not an adopted staffing standard.

28

Data notes

  1. Reporting coverage. FY2027 runs July 2026 through June 2027. July–September 2026 are reported; October 2026 onward have not been entered in the workbook.
  2. Handle-time entry. Handle times entered as hours:minutes in parts of the workbook were read as minutes:seconds, consistent with the system-calculated totals.
  3. July web intakes. 2,098 on the monthly tab versus 2,077 on the staff-sharing tabs; this dashboard uses the monthly tab.
  4. Updated workbook (10/06/26). Core July–September figures are unchanged. New data points added: team-level handle time, BRI enrollments and caseload (new, discharged, clients served), UniteUs referrals by Special Projects staff, CCSP DON-Rs and rescreens by staff, ePRO updating compliance, monthly trend narratives, and September ePRO and transitions counts.
  5. August dispositions. Elevated “not dispositioned” calls reflect a disposition set-up error, since corrected.
  6. September status time. The status-time report omits Ready time; September ePRO record counts and transitions activity are now entered.
  7. Team averages. Recalculated from team values; the workbook’s August team-average calls per hour (62) appears to be an entry error.
